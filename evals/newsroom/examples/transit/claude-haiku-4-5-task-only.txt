# Saturday Loop Service: What the Proposed Changes Mean for Passengers

Bay Cross Transit's advisory board has recommended reducing Saturday Loop service from six to four departures each day. However, the change remains a proposal—not an approved schedule—and several steps must complete before any change could take effect.

## Current and Proposed Schedule

The Saturday Loop currently departs the station at 08:00, 10:00, 12:00, 14:00, 16:00, and 18:00, running through Yard Square and Cliff Terrace before returning. The board's recommendation would retain departures at 08:00, 10:00, 16:00, and 18:00—removing the midday 12:00 and 14:00 trips.

The removal of these two departures creates a six-hour gap between the 10:00 and 16:00 trips. This spacing differs from the journey duration: riders on the Saturday Loop are not traveling for six hours. The gap refers to how long passengers would wait if they arrived at the station between these two departure times.

## Why This Change Is Proposed

Bay Cross Transit's board cited driver availability during the middle shift as the reason for the recommendation. The vote passed five to three. The board chair stated in the formal minutes: "This is a recommendation, not a timetable."

## The Access Concern

The Bay Cross Access Circle, which represents passengers with access needs, submitted a written comment during the board review. Their statement: "A six-hour gap would change whether a same-day visit is practical." The group urged planners to consider passengers who need a midday return trip, not only the first and last service of the day.

## What Must Happen Before Implementation

The proposal cannot move forward without completing these steps:

**Staffing Plan**: The operating team must first provide a staffing plan to the funding committee.

**Funding Committee Approval**: The committee is scheduled to consider the recommendation on May 22, 2026. No decision has yet been recorded.

**Public Comment Period**: Passengers can submit comments through May 15, 2026, at https://example.org/bay-cross/saturday-comments. Public comments are submissions for consideration, not votes that determine the outcome.

**Public Notice**: If approved, Bay Cross Transit must provide at least 14 days of public notice before any new timetable begins.

## Right Now

The current six-departure Saturday schedule remains in effect. Stop panels display the current timetable and will continue to do so until an officially approved replacement is issued. No start date for any schedule change has been announced.
