# Draft: Bay Cross Transit Saturday Loop Proposal Explained

**What's Being Proposed**

Bay Cross Transit's advisory board has recommended reducing Saturday departures on the Loop from six to four. The existing schedule leaves the station at 8 a.m., 10 a.m., 12 p.m., 2 p.m., 4 p.m., and 6 p.m. The proposed schedule would remove the noon and 2 p.m. trips, leaving departures at 8 a.m., 10 a.m., 4 p.m., and 6 p.m. The Loop continues to serve Yard Square and Cliff Terrace before returning to the station. No changes to stops or fares are proposed.

This is a recommendation, not a timetable. The current six-departure schedule remains in effect, and no start date has been announced for any change.

**Why the Change?**

The board paper cites driver availability for the middle shift as the reason for the proposal. It does not specify how many drivers the current schedule requires or what savings or service gains would result.

**The Access Concern**

The gap between the 10 a.m. and 4 p.m. departures—six hours with no service—prompted written comment from the Bay Cross Access Circle. The group noted that "a six-hour gap would change whether a same-day visit is practical," and asked the planning team to consider passengers who need a midday return option rather than only the first and last departures of the day. The comment is part of the public record but does not determine whether the change moves forward.

**What Happens Next**

The approval process has several remaining steps:

The funding committee is scheduled to consider the proposal on May 22, 2026. Before that vote, the operating team must supply a staffing plan. That plan is required information for the committee's decision.

Passengers and others can submit written comments through May 15, 2026, at https://example.org/bay-cross/saturday-comments. Public comments are submissions for consideration, not votes. The funding committee will weigh all input before deciding.

If the committee approves the change, Bay Cross Transit must post at least 14 days of public notice before the new timetable begins. Stop panels throughout the service area will display the current six-departure timetable until an authorized replacement is issued.

**Current Information**

For now, plan travel using the existing schedule: departures at 8 a.m., 10 a.m., 12 p.m., 2 p.m., 4 p.m., and 6 p.m. Bay Cross Transit will announce any changes through its website and service notifications once approval is complete.
