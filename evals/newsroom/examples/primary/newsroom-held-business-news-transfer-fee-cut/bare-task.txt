Wayfern Transfer to Cut Keldra Cash-Transfer Fee to $2, but Exchange Rate Stays Separate

Wayfern Transfer said Tuesday it will cut the fee on qualifying cash transfers to Keldra from $4 to $2 starting Sept. 15, though company officials cautioned that the change applies only to the transfer fee itself and does not alter the exchange rate recipients ultimately receive.

The lower fee applies to cash-funded transfers of $100 through $500, inclusive, sent at one of Wayfern's eight local agency counters, the company said. That range does not include the fee, which is charged in addition to the amount sent rather than subtracted from it. Transfers made through Wayfern's separate online product are not affected and continue to follow their own fee schedule.

Using a Sept. 1 reference quote, the company said a $200 transfer would cost a sender $202 under the new fee, down from $204 currently. At that exchange rate — 9.8 Keldran luma per dollar, compared with a reference rate of 10 — the recipient would receive 1,960 luma either way, since the fee reduction affects only what the sender pays, not the conversion. Wayfern said its currency-conversion margin is unchanged by the announcement, and that the Sept. 1 rate is a dated example not guaranteed to be available Sept. 15 or afterward.

"The transfer fee is lower. We are not saying every part of the exchange has become cheaper," said Iona Pell, a Wayfern manager, who attributed the fee cut to a renegotiated agency contract. She did not disclose the contract's commercial terms.

Wayfern said recipients pay no additional Wayfern collection fee through the payout partners covered by the offer, though the company did not name those partners. The record does not address whether banks or intermediaries outside that network might impose their own charges.

The company said it will display both the amount charged to the sender and the payout amount before a customer confirms a transfer, and that expected payout timing remains the next business day, subject to existing identity and transaction checks. The announcement includes no end date for the lower fee, but Wayfern said that does not amount to a guarantee locking in future exchange rates or other fees.

Wayfern processed 12,400 transfers on the Keldra route last month, a count of transactions rather than of individual senders, according to the company. Wayfern opened its first local counter, inside a travel shop, in 2010.

Reni Moss, a customer who sends money for a relative's household expenses, said she uses an agency counter because she prefers a printed receipt. She said the fee reduction is welcome but not the only thing she watches.

"The fee on my side matters, but I also ask what number appears on the other side," Moss said.

Wayfern's announcement did not include industry-wide comparisons, and no ranking establishing Wayfern as the cheapest transfer provider to Keldra was available. Customers weighing the new fee against the total cost of sending money may want to compare the payout amount, not just the fee, before choosing a service.

The fee change takes effect Sept. 15. Wayfern said further details will be available at its agency counters and will appear in the payment confirmation screen before customers complete a transfer.
