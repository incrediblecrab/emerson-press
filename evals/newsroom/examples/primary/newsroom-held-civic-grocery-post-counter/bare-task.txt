Headline: Mallow Fen Post Counter Relocating to Reed Basket Grocery Oct. 1

MALLOW FEN — The village's post counter will move from its longtime High Row location into the Reed Basket grocery store starting Oct. 1, as the current site's lease expires without renewal, the Mallow Post cooperative said.

The lease on the High Row counter ends Sept. 30, and the landlord declined to extend it, cooperative manager Fenna Orr said. Rather than close the counter, the cooperative arranged to rent space inside Reed Basket, about 600 yards away on the village's existing bus route.

"We are changing the room around the service, not asking shoppers to hand their mail to any cashier," Orr said.

Under a three-year agreement, the cooperative will rent a counter area and a secured parcel room from the grocer. The grocer will pay for a partition being installed to separate the postal waiting line from the grocery checkout line; the cooperative will supply postal equipment and staff training.

Two current postal employees will transfer to the new site, and the cooperative said no jobs will be cut as a result of the move.

Postal hours will remain 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Monday through Friday and 9 a.m. to noon Saturday. Those hours will not be extended to match the grocery's longer opening times, because postal transactions require separately trained staff, the cooperative said.

Grocer Dalen Wick said he welcomes the added foot traffic but stressed that the two operations must stay distinct.

"Extra customers are welcome, but this only works if someone buying a stamp is not trapped behind a weekly shop," Wick said.

Postal workers, not grocery cashiers, will continue to handle undelivered parcels and customers' collection paperwork, and services will include letter posting, parcel collection and postage sales. The move will not add banking or money-transfer services, the cooperative said.

The new location offers step-free entry, unlike the current counter, which requires customers to climb two exterior steps.

The parish transport group has asked for a bench outside the new site, noting that some bus connections leave riders waiting past the counter's closing time. The cooperative said no decision has been made on the bench request, nor on any later extension of postal hours.

Uncollected parcels from the High Row counter will be transferred to the new secured room as part of the move. The outdoor posting box at the old site will remain in place and continue to be emptied on its existing schedule.

The cooperative said it will post notices on parcel-collection cards and at both locations explaining the change of address.

The current postal room occupies a former tailor's shop; a wooden fitting-room door from that earlier business is still used for the staff cupboard.

The cooperative described the move as a way to keep a post counter operating in the village rather than losing it altogether, but it offered no guarantee about service continuing beyond the three-year agreement with Reed Basket. The packet on the arrangement did not disclose the rent involved or any projection of additional grocery sales expected from hosting the postal counter.
