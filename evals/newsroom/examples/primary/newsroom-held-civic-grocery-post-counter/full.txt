**Mallow Fen postal counter to relocate into grocery store**

MALLOW FEN — The village's postal counter will move into the Reed Basket grocery store on Oct. 1, after the landlord of its current High Row location declined to renew the lease, which ends Sept. 30, the Mallow Post cooperative said.

The new counter will sit about 600 yards from the old one, on the village's existing local bus route, according to the cooperative.

Postal hours will remain 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Monday through Friday and 9 a.m. to noon Saturday. The grocery's longer opening hours will not extend those hours because the counter requires separately trained staff, the cooperative said.

Two postal employees will transfer to the new site, and the cooperative said it plans no job cuts tied to the move. Under a three-year agreement, the cooperative will rent a counter area and a secured parcel room from the grocer.

Postal workers, not grocery cashiers, will continue to handle undelivered parcels and customers' collection documents, the cooperative said. The relocated counter will keep letter posting, parcel collection and postage sales but will not add banking or money-transfer services.

"We are changing the room around the service, not asking shoppers to hand their mail to any cashier," cooperative manager Fenna Orr said.

The grocery's entrance has no steps, unlike the current counter, which requires customers to climb two exterior steps. An internal partition is being installed to separate the postal waiting line from the grocery checkout line. The grocer will pay for the partition, while the cooperative will supply postal equipment and staff training, the cooperative said.

Grocer Dalen Wick said the arrangement depends on keeping the lines separate. "Extra customers are welcome, but this only works if someone buying a stamp is not trapped behind a weekly shop," he said.

The parish transport group has asked for a bench outside the new site, saying some bus connections leave passengers waiting beyond the counter's closing time. The cooperative has not decided on the request or on extending postal hours later in the year.

The outdoor posting box at the current location will remain in place and continue to be emptied on its existing schedule. Any parcels left uncollected at the High Row counter when it closes will be transferred to the new secured room, the cooperative said. Notices explaining the address change will be placed on parcel-collection cards and at both premises.

The current postal room occupies a former tailor's shop; its wooden fitting-room door is still used for the staff cupboard.

The cooperative said the move allows it to avoid closing the village counter altogether, but it has made no guarantee about service once the three-year agreement with the grocer expires.

The reporting packet supplied for this account does not disclose the rent the cooperative will pay or any forecast of additional grocery sales tied to hosting the counter.
