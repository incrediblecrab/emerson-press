**Bramble Quay Library adds after-hours tool-return lockers at 4 branches**

Bramble Quay's Library Board voted Sept. 3 to install after-hours tool-return lockers at four of its six branches, giving borrowers a way to drop off items like drills and sewing machines without waiting for staffed hours.

The service is scheduled to begin Oct. 5. Installation work has started, but the lockers are not yet available for use.

Each of the four participating branches will get 16 compartments, for 64 across the system. Currently, all tool returns must go to a staffed desk.

The $128,000 project, covering installation and the first year of maintenance, is financed by a designated foundation grant. The library estimates annual maintenance after that first year at $12,000, a cost the grant does not cover. A future operating allocation would be needed to sustain the service long-term.

"Returning something should not require taking time off work," library director Osa Merrin said in the announcement.

Under the plan, borrowers will reserve a compartment sized for their item through their library account or ask a branch employee to make the reservation. The system will record the deposit time, which will determine whether an item was returned by its deadline even if staff do not inspect it until the next day.

An item will not immediately clear from a borrower's account after it is placed in a locker. An employee must first check its parts and condition. Staff will empty and inspect the compartments before each participating branch opens on its next staffed day.

The lockers will handle returns only; borrowing a tool will still require the existing in-branch checkout process. Oversized equipment and items in leaking containers are excluded and must be returned during staffed hours. Borrowers may return a loan to any of the four equipped branches, regardless of where they checked it out.

The two branches not included in the first phase lack suitable space under their current building leases, according to the library.

A tenants association near one of the excluded branches has asked the board to publish options for residents who cannot easily travel to another branch. "An extra option across town is not the same option for someone carrying a sewing machine," association secretary Devra Senn said.

Printed locker instructions will be available at all six branches, including the two that will not have lockers.

The board has asked staff for a January 2027 review covering compartment availability, incomplete returns and requests made through branch employees. Library officials said the review will not begin with a target for reducing staff positions, and the approved plan contains no staffing cuts.

The library's tool collection began with donated garden equipment; replacement items are now purchased through the library's regular collection budget.

The library has not yet collected usage data for the tool-return service and has not estimated how many borrowers might change their return habits once the lockers open.
