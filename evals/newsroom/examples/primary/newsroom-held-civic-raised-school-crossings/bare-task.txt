Terncross Council Approves $410,000 Plan for Three Raised School Crossings

TERNCROSS — The Terncross Council has accepted a $310,000 county grant to help build three raised pedestrian crossings near primary schools, with the city adding $100,000 to bring the approved project budget to $410,000, officials said.

The crossings will be built outside Willow Gate School, at the west approach to Bell Lane School, and between Ash Court School and its playing field. The transport team said it chose the sites because they connect existing walking routes, rather than simply targeting the locations with the most complaints.

Each design raises the crossing surface to the level of the adjoining footway and includes revised drainage, according to city documents. The grant funds construction and design but does not pay for additional crossing-guard wages, officials said.

Two of the three sites currently have crossing guards, and the approved project does not remove either staffed position. A guard vacancy at the third site remains unfilled, with recruitment proceeding separately from construction procurement.

"The new surface and the person helping children across are different parts of the route," transport engineer Sela Dorn said.

Construction is planned for the February 2027 school break, though a contractor and final work sequence have not yet been selected. The city said it expects short lane restrictions during the work and will publish detailed arrangements once a contractor's plan is approved. The existing marked crossings will remain in place until each replacement is ready for use.

The grant application includes written accounts of difficult crossings but not a verified count of crashes or near misses, and the project packet contains no completed safety evaluation or prediction of a specific reduction in collisions.

A parents group welcomed the connected walking route but asked the city to fill the vacant guard position before the new crossings open.

"A construction date is not an answer to who will be standing there next Monday," said parent representative Evin Rell.

Bus operators have asked that the raised surfaces be reviewed with their vehicles in mind as detailed design continues.

The county has required a post-opening assessment of access and drainage before releasing the final portion of its grant. City officials noted that assessment will take place after construction and does not indicate the crossings already meet every intended outcome.

Schools will distribute the city's work notices through both printed newsletters and existing electronic mailing lists. At Willow Gate, the school's older wooden crossing signs have been offered to its drama department once they are removed.

City officials said the funding covers only these three locations; other requested crossings remain part of the city's wider transport programme and were not addressed by this grant.

No timeline for filling the vacant guard position was included in the materials reviewed. The council did not indicate when a contractor selection is expected, though it said further details, including lane-restriction schedules, would be released once construction plans are finalized.
