Bay Cross Transit passengers are being told to treat a proposed Saturday service change as exactly that: a proposal, not a new timetable.

The advisory board has recommended cutting the Saturday Loop from six departures each Saturday to four, and the recommendation passed by five votes to three. In the current timetable, buses leave the station at 08:00, 10:00, 12:00, 14:00, 16:00 and 18:00. Under the recommendation, the Saturday departures would be 08:00, 10:00, 16:00 and 18:00.

That means the most noticeable change would be the removal of the 12:00 and 14:00 departures. The midday gap refers to the spacing between departures, not the length of the ride. The Saturday Loop would still run the same route — from the station through Yard Square and Cliff Terrace and back to the station — and the proposal says there would be no change to stops, fares or weekday and Sunday service.

The board paper says the reason for the proposal is driver availability for the middle shift. But the change is not approved yet. The funding committee must approve it before any new timetable can take effect, and the operating team must supply a staffing plan before that approval can be given. The committee is scheduled to consider the proposal on 22 May 2026, and no outcome has been recorded.

Bay Cross Transit has also not announced a start date for any changed timetable. Under the service’s process, at least 14 days of public notice are required before an approved timetable change begins. Stop panels will keep showing the current timetable until an authorised replacement is issued.

An access concern has already been raised. The Bay Cross Access Circle said in its written comment: “A six-hour gap would change whether a same-day visit is practical.” The group asked planners to consider passengers who need a midday return rather than only the first and last departures. The packet does not include boarding counts, financial savings or an alternative midday shuttle.

Public comments may be submitted until 15 May 2026 at https://example.org/bay-cross/saturday-comments. Those comments are submissions for consideration, not ballots that determine the timetable.

As the board chair said in the supplied minutes: “This is a recommendation, not a timetable.”
