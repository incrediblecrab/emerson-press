# Bay Cross Transit Saturday Service: What's Being Proposed, and What Happens Next

Bay Cross Transit's advisory board has recommended reducing Saturday service on the Loop route from six departures each day to four, citing driver availability constraints during the middle shift. The recommendation, which passed 5–3 on [date from minutes], remains under review and has not yet taken effect. The current timetable—with departures at 08:00, 10:00, 12:00, 14:00, 16:00, and 18:00—continues for all Saturday trips.

## The Proposed Change

If approved, the new timetable would depart at 08:00, 10:00, 16:00, and 18:00, eliminating the 12:00 and 14:00 departures. The Loop follows the same route through Yard Square and Cliff Terrace in either scenario, with no changes proposed to stops or fares. The recommendation affects Saturday service only; weekday and Sunday schedules would remain unchanged.

## The Midday Gap and Access Questions

The removal of midday departures creates a six-hour interval between the 10:00 and 16:00 trips—the time passengers would wait for the next service, not the journey duration itself. That spacing prompted written comment from the Bay Cross Access Circle, which stated: "A six-hour gap would change whether a same-day visit is practical." The group asked planners to consider passengers who need a midday return, rather than relying solely on morning and evening service.

## What Must Happen Before Changes Take Effect

The proposal cannot become official without multiple approvals. The operating team must first submit a detailed staffing plan addressing how the reduced service would function. The funding committee is scheduled to review the proposal on 22 May 2026, though no decision has yet been recorded.

Before any approved timetable can begin, Bay Cross Transit's procedures require at least 14 days of public notice. Stop panels will display the current six-departure schedule until an authorised replacement is formally issued.

## How to Weigh In

Public comment is open through 15 May 2026 at https://example.org/bay-cross/saturday-comments. Submissions received during this window are considered by planners; comments are input for decision-makers, not votes that directly determine the final schedule.

## The Current Status

As the board chair noted in the meeting minutes, "This is a recommendation, not a timetable." The advisory board's vote supports studying the change, but no implementation date has been announced, and approval from both the operating team and funding committee remains pending.
