Bay Cross Transit’s advisory board has recommended cutting the Saturday Loop from six departures to four, but the existing timetable remains in place and no start date for any change has been announced.

Under the current Saturday schedule, buses leave the station at 08:00, 10:00, 12:00, 14:00, 16:00 and 18:00. The proposed timetable would keep the first two departures and the last two departures, but remove the midday trips at 12:00 and 14:00. That means the gap in the schedule would be between departures, not in how long the route takes to complete. The Loop still runs from the station through Yard Square and Cliff Terrace and back to the station, and the proposal does not change the route, stops or fares.

The board approved the recommendation by five votes to three. Board chair said in the minutes: “This is a recommendation, not a timetable.” The funding committee must still approve the change before a new schedule can take effect, and the operating team must supply a staffing plan before the committee can act on the proposal. The committee is scheduled to consider it on 22 May 2026, but no outcome has been recorded.

The board paper says the reason for proposing the reduction is driver availability for the middle shift. The packet does not include boarding counts or any estimate of financial savings, and it does not offer an alternative midday shuttle.

Passenger access concerns were raised in a written comment from the Bay Cross Access Circle. The group said: “A six-hour gap would change whether a same-day visit is practical.” It asked planners to consider passengers who need a midday return, not only those using the first and last departures. The comment is part of the public record, but public comments are submissions for consideration, not ballots that decide the timetable.

If approved, the change would still need to clear the service’s notice requirement. Bay Cross Transit’s process requires at least 14 days of public notice before an approved timetable change can begin. Stop panels will continue to show the current timetable until an authorised replacement is issued.

Public comments may be submitted until 15 May 2026 at https://example.org/bay-cross/saturday-comments. The recommendation applies only to Saturday service and does not address weekday or Sunday operations.
