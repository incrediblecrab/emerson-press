**Saturday Loop Timetable Change Proposed for Consideration**

Bay Cross Transit's advisory board has recommended cutting Saturday service on the Loop from six departures to four, citing driver availability during the middle of the day. The funding committee is scheduled to consider the proposal on May 22, but no decision has been made. The current timetable remains in effect.

Under the proposal, Saturday Loop departures from the station would shift from 8 a.m., 10 a.m., noon, 2 p.m., 4 p.m., and 6 p.m. to 8 a.m., 10 a.m., 4 p.m., and 6 p.m. The recommendation eliminates the noon and 2 p.m. departures, creating a six-hour gap between the 10 a.m. and 4 p.m. departures. This is a gap in service timing, not in how long a trip takes. The Loop route itself — through Yard Square and Cliff Terrace — would not change, nor would fares or stops.

The board voted 5-3 to recommend the change. "This is a recommendation, not a timetable," the board chair said in the official minutes, noting that several approval steps must follow before passengers see any change.

**What comes next**

The operating team must supply a staffing plan explaining how the reduced schedule would work before the funding committee can vote on approval. Even if approved, at least 14 days of public notice must run before the new timetable takes effect. Stop panels will continue to display the current schedule until an authorized replacement is issued.

Passengers can submit public comments by May 15 at https://example.org/bay-cross/saturday-comments. Comments are submitted for the committee's consideration; they are not a vote on whether the service changes.

**Access concern raised**

The Bay Cross Access Circle, an advocacy group, submitted a comment on the proposal. "A six-hour gap would change whether a same-day visit is practical," the group wrote, noting that passengers who need a midday return — rather than relying only on the first and last departures — would be affected by the reduction.

The board paper identifying the reason for the proposal mentions driver availability for the middle shift but contains no boarding counts or estimates of how many passengers currently use the noon and 2 p.m. departures.

The Saturday Loop change would affect Saturday service only. No changes are proposed to weekday or Sunday schedules.
