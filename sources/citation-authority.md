### Narrow Details Confirmed

**Chicago author thresholds:** in the public journal-article guidance, two authors are both named. For three or more, a note or author-date in-text citation uses the first author followed by *et al.* The bibliography/reference list names all authors up to six; with more than six, it gives the first three followed by *et al.* Do not confuse the short citation's threshold with the reference list's threshold. Other cases and exceptions require the relevant entry.

**Chicago optional fields and locators:** the public notes/bibliography guide says publication place is no longer required in ordinary book citations and chapter page ranges are no longer required in bibliography entries. That is not a prohibition on including useful or venue-required information. A journal-article note uses the cited pages; the bibliography normally uses the whole article's range or article identifier. Thus the two forms differ in more than punctuation.

**MLA locators:** use an available source location to direct readers to the specific passage. The overview also avoids repeating the author in parentheses when already supplied in the sentence. As a house accuracy safeguard, never fabricate page numbers for an unpaginated source. Choosing a supported alternative locator remains source- and style-dependent.

## Print Editions Are Not Living AI Guidance

MLA's [August 2025 updated guidance](https://style.mla.org/citing-generative-ai-updated-revised/) is separate from the 2021 handbook. It recommends identifying the model/version, tool provider and generation date, preferring a stable shareable conversation URL and using the tool URL if the tool lacks a stable share-link feature. This does not establish a universal rule for confidential or otherwise restricted conversations. It does not treat the tool as an author and recommends consulting and citing underlying sources directly rather than relying on an AI summary.

That public update was read; the examples are not reproduced here. APA's September 2025 formats were not verified in this pass and must not be inferred from MLA, from the print edition alone or from the older 2023 ChatGPT guidance. Citation, disclosure of assistance and permission to use AI are separate questions governed by the applicable venue.

Unless a specific public update is identified here, an AI example in a consuming module is not evidence of a fresh audit of that publisher's current AI-specific rules.

## Overrides and Integration

- Record the requested edition and the venue's adopted rules. Follow explicit journal, court or local requirements within their scope; identify conflicts instead of silently mixing systems.
- Verify that an identifier resolves to the intended source and that the source supports the proposition. Correct punctuation cannot repair a wrong authority or an invented locator.
- For `domain/legal.md`, this dossier supports **citation mechanics only**. It does not establish substantive law, precedential weight, jurisdiction, procedural rights or the validity of a legal conclusion.
- The seven consumers link back to this dossier and distinguish its narrow checks from unresolved rules. Those reciprocal links support provenance tracking; they do not certify an entire module or replace checking a disputed entry.
