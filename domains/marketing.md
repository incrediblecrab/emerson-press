# Domain: Marketing

Copy that sells. Landing pages, product descriptions, campaigns, press releases.

Marketing advocates for an interested party. Its claims should still help the reader make an informed decision. Incentives can conflict in many kinds of writing; this is not the only genre subject to accuracy duties or regulation.

Hold adequate support for an objective claim before publication. U.S. FTC substantiation principles are an important orientation, not a universal rule for every jurisdiction or claim type. Evidence may be published, linked, supplied on request or retained internally. Do not invent it or assume that advertising is uniquely accountable for having it.

**Puffery is not a blanket legal safe harbor or a quality verdict.** Context can turn a boast into an implied measurable claim. Playful brand language can work, while an empty superlative may tell the reader little. A specific statistic is not automatically more persuasive, and a plausible invented one is worse than an honest general description.

Fabricated customer reviews and testimonials can violate U.S. rules, including 16 CFR Part 465, as well as deceive readers. Verify current applicability, jurisdiction and sector requirements rather than treating this writing module as legal advice. Clearly labeled internal fictional examples are not real customer evidence and must not be published as such.

## Detect

### The claim

- An objective claim with no evidence behind it: performance, speed, savings, health, safety, efficacy, environmental benefit.
- A comparative with no comparison — *faster*, *more affordable*, *better results* — missing what it was measured against, how, and when.
- A statistic without its necessary source, scope or period. Round numbers can be real; check the underlying record rather than inferring fabrication from neatness.
- Testimonials that are composite, edited past recognition, incentivized without disclosure, or generated.
- Results presented as typical when they are the best case.
- *Eco-friendly*, *green*, *sustainable*, *non-toxic*, *natural* standing alone. General environmental benefit claims are effectively unsupportable unqualified.
- Health, structure-function, or efficacy language on a product not held to a medical standard.
- A material disclosure that is easy to miss or appears too late for the decision. Assess the actual surface and applicable requirements rather than one universal font-size formula.

### The register

- Named constructions: *It's not just X — it's Y. Say goodbye to X. Meet your new X. Supercharge your workflow. Unlock the power of X. In seconds, not hours.*
- Default intensifiers: *seamless, effortless, blazing fast, game-changing, revolutionary, next-level, robust, enterprise-grade.*
- Empty claims of distinction that survive a competitor-name swap. Useful general instructions or explanations are not defects merely because they also apply elsewhere.
- Dismissal as a personality: *no more X theater*, *X, reimagined*.
- Ordinals used as an ordering claim — 01 / 02 / 03 over items in no sequence.
- Punchy triads set as paragraphs. *Fast. Beautiful. Yours.*
- Benefits with no mechanism. *Save hours every week* with no account of what the product does.
- Manufactured urgency: countdowns that reset, scarcity that is not scarce.
- Pricing revealed a step at a time, or a subscription whose cancellation path is harder than its signup.

## Write

Give the page or campaign a clear purpose and make supported claims. Do not invent a customer sample, benchmark, causal benefit or date to turn a vague pitch into a measurable one. Flag unsubstantiated draft claims before publication.

Name the comparison. Against what, measured how, on what population, when.

Attribute every number in the copy itself where it is load-bearing, and keep the substantiation file current for everything else. If the number cannot be sourced, cut it — never round it into plausibility.

Say what the product does before saying what it means for the reader. A benefit with no mechanism under it is the claim a reader discounts fastest, because they have read a thousand of them.

Use real, authorized customer statements without changing their meaning, with relevant material connections disclosed clearly. Do not generate an experience and attribute it to a customer, expand an abbreviated name into an invented identity or manufacture a no-compensation statement.

Qualify environmental claims to the specific attribute and the specific extent: *packaging is 60% post-consumer recycled fiber*, not *sustainable packaging*.

Make material disclosures conspicuous and close enough to qualify the claim before the decision. Comparable visual prominence is a house safeguard, not a claim that every law requires identical type size and weight. Follow the actual jurisdiction, medium and sector rules.

State material limitations and unsuitable uses honestly. Do not invent exclusions or promise a conversion, trust or review-score benefit from doing so.

Delete the intensifier and see whether the sentence still makes a claim. If it does not, the intensifier was doing all the work and the claim was empty.

## Mechanics

AP-based prose is this module's default; an explicit house guide or venue can override its mechanics. Source links or formal references are available where needed. The chosen citation format does not establish legal compliance.

- Attribution in the sentence for load-bearing numbers, with the date.
- Every claim mapped to a substantiation file: what supports it, who produced the support, when, and when it expires.
- Disclosures clear, prominent and timely for the actual claim, surface and applicable rules.
- Material connections stated in plain words, not a hashtag buried in a tag block.
- Prices complete on first appearance: total, term, renewal, and what is excluded.
- Trademarks and required legal lines carried where the jurisdiction demands, without letting them substitute for a disclosure that has to be conspicuous.
- Claims dated and re-verified on a schedule. A substantiated claim decays as the product changes.

## Formats

### Landing page

One page, one stranger, one question: is this for me?

This module governs the **claim**; `domains/user-interface/website.md` governs the **page** — hero, kicker, badge, stat row, display type, and the surface conventions that go with them. Compose both. Where they overlap, the substantiation floor here is not negotiable by layout.

Lead with what the product does, in the reader's words, specifically enough that the wrong reader can leave. State the mechanism, then the evidence, then the ask. One primary action.

A page can fail to distinguish the product when it relies on interchangeable claims or unsupported metrics. A real three-feature grid can be useful; no conversion effect is established merely by recognizing a template.

### Product copy

Short and decision-supporting. The reader is comparing.

Give specifications rather than adjectives: dimensions, materials, capacity, compatibility, what is in the box. *Fits a 16-inch laptop* beats *spacious* because it settles the question.

State limitations plainly so the reader can judge fit. Do not claim that doing so necessarily improves return rates or review scores without evidence.

Say who it is not for.

### Campaign email

Permission-based, competing with a full inbox, and the only marketing format the reader can end permanently with one click.

Subject line describes the contents truthfully. A subject that misrepresents to earn an open trades a long relationship for a single impression.

One offer, one action, the terms visible before the button, and an unsubscribe that works in one step.

Segment on behavior rather than writing to everyone and calling it a campaign.

### Press release

Marketing prose wearing journalism's clothes, which is what makes it hazardous and what makes it useful. A reporter will read it looking for a reason not to.

Lead with what is actually new, in a sentence that could run as written. Put the verifiable facts high: what, who, when, how much, available where.

Attribute quotations to their actual authorized source, including written statements. Remove empty repetition without inventing a more vivid quote. Make consequential claims checkable and provide a real contact route when supplied.

`domains/press.md` describes what the receiving newsroom does with it. Writing to that standard is the whole technique.

## Evidence

The legacy `evidence_floor: 4` emphasizes adequate, examined support. The required evidence depends on the claim: a specification, price, performance comparison, treatment claim and recycled-content statement do not all require the same study design. Health, safety and environmental claims can have additional scientific or regulatory requirements.

Support must exist when a claim is published, not be invented to fill a substantiation file. A draft can identify evidence still needed; it must not be represented as ready to publish. Published references and internal records can both contribute, within their real scope.

Fabrication is an editorial failure and can create legal liability. This module does not decide the legal status of every hypothetical claim.

The floor is not a courtesy the writer extends, so no reader gets to decline it. A `domains/education-level/` tier changes the words on the page. The substantiation file behind them stays exactly the same size.

## Boundaries

Against `domains/user-interface/`. The clearest split in the repository and a recent one. `domains/user-interface/website.md` owns the marketing page's surface; this module owns the truth of what the page says, in every medium — email, print, script, packaging, release. A tell listed in both places is listed once as a page convention and once as a claim about the world.

With medical safeguards. Keep marketing as the primary genre for health-related promotion, and add `domains/medical.md`'s `## Safeguards` through `--safeguard medical`. Those constraints apply to clinical meaning and risk without importing manuscript formatting. A mechanism, testimonial or animal study does not by itself establish the claimed benefit in people.

Against `press`. A press release is marketing; the story written from it is journalism. Copy that mimics reportorial form to borrow its credibility — the native ad, the sponsored post — must be labeled, and the label is a disclosure subject to the conspicuousness standard above.

Against `technical`. Product documentation drifts into this register whenever the organization that sells the thing also documents it. `technical` wins there absolutely: a document a stuck reader is using is not a sales surface.

With `stop-the-slop/anti-slop.md`. Core's patterns are review aids, not universal bans or authorship evidence. This module adds claim-substantiation and disclosure concerns; a legitimate familiar phrase can stay.

With `domains/education-level/`. Use the actual reader's needs, not credentials as a proxy for vulnerability or expertise. Do not exploit limited familiarity or remove a material qualifier in the name of simplicity. Additional requirements for particular audiences need their own legal check.

## Examples

These independent synthetic records supply the product facts. They do not establish real customer evidence, legal compliance or measured conversion effects.

**Puffery is not evidence**

**Supplied facts:** The fictional product provides shared boards showing each task's owner and due date. No sprint-speed study, account sample or claim that it replaces a standup is supplied.

> The world's most powerful project management platform. Seamlessly supercharge your team's workflow and unlock unprecedented productivity.

> Shared boards show each task's owner and due date.

**Numbers assembled to look like numbers**

**Supplied facts:** The approved fictional record lists 1,847 paying teams as of June 30, 2026 and 99.94% uptime for the 12 months ending then. Internal tests give median import times of 4.2 seconds on the new engine and 21 seconds on the previous one for the same 50,000-row file and test configuration. No rating rank, customer-satisfaction measure or external monitoring provider is supplied.

> 10,000+ happy customers · 99.9% uptime · 5x faster · #1 rated

> 1,847 paying teams as of June 30, 2026. Recorded uptime of 99.94% for the 12 months ending then. In internal tests using the same 50,000-row file and configuration, median import time was 4.2 seconds on the new engine versus 21 seconds on the previous one.

**A comparative with nothing to compare to**

**Supplied facts:** The supplied manufacturer specification rates the battery at 800 charge cycles to 80% of original capacity. No comparison product, industry norm, independent laboratory report or IEC certification is supplied. The draft still needs its rating's substantiation and test conditions checked.

> Our batteries last significantly longer.

> Rated for 800 charge cycles to 80% of original capacity.

*Verification note: confirm the rating's substantiation and test conditions before publication. No comparative or independent-certification claim is supported.*

**Environmental claim standing alone**

**Supplied facts:** The supplier record says the paperboard used in the mailer is 100% post-consumer recycled material; the inner sleeve has no recycled content. Municipal collection acceptance and comparative environmental effects have not been established.

> Eco-friendly, sustainable packaging. Good for the planet.

> The mailer's paperboard is 100% post-consumer recycled material. The inner sleeve contains no recycled material. Check local recycling rules.

**A testimonial that should not exist**

**Supplied facts:** No actual customer statement, identity, permission or compensation record supports the proposed testimonial. The task is review, not invention of a more convincing customer.

> "This product completely transformed our business. I can't imagine working without it!" — Sarah M., Marketing Director

*Remove the unsupported testimonial. Obtain an actual authorized statement and the relevant connection/disclosure information before proposing a replacement.*

**Register borrowed from a template**

**Supplied facts:** The fictional notebook is A5 with 192 pages of 120 gsm paper and a lay-flat binding. It has no perforation, pocket or elastic and costs $18. No ink-performance test or manufacturing-cost explanation is supplied.

> It's not just a notebook — it's your second brain. Say goodbye to scattered thoughts. Meet your new creative companion. Fast. Beautiful. Yours.

> A5, 192 pages, 120 gsm paper. Lies flat. No perforation, pocket or elastic. $18.
