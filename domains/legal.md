# Domain: Legal

Memos, briefs, contracts and client letters. Use `domains/citation/bluebook22.md` when the court, venue or task requests it. The evidence dossier supports citation mechanics only, not substantive law, current authority or the validity of a legal conclusion.

Distinguish a prediction, a statement of authority and a negotiated obligation. *The court would likely hold* can express a necessary limit rather than a stylistic weakness. A contract's commitment should be clear, but an uncertain legal outcome must not become certain through copyediting.

Make uncertainty informative when the evidence permits: identify the governing facts, open questions and relevant conditions. Do not invent a numerical probability, deadline, record or legal exception simply to make a hedge more concrete.

Legal citations convey authority and the relationship between a source and a proposition. Distinguish binding from persuasive authority, holding from dictum and a current rule from a superseded one. A signal must reflect the source's actual support, not how confidently the writer wants to sound.

U.S. model-rule examples below are orientation, not a statement that every jurisdiction adopts the same rule. Check the governing law, local court rules and applicable professional duties. This module does not replace legal research or qualified review.

## Detect

### Substance

- Outcome stated as certain: *we will win, the court will hold, this is clearly unenforceable.*
- A hedge that hides a known material condition or fails to say what information is missing.
- Advice untethered from the facts and the jurisdiction it depends on.
- The bad cases missing from a memo. A predictive document that only found helpful authority did not predict anything.
- Counsel that answers a different, easier question than the one asked.
- A recommendation that leaves an important next step unclear. An unknown deadline or cost must not be invented.

### Authority

- A case cited past its holding, or a quotation lifted from dicta and used as though it were the rule.
- Authority not validated for subsequent history — reversed, vacated, superseded, abrogated, or overruled.
- A persuasive court cited as though binding, or no forum identified at all.
- A citation with no pincite behind a specific proposition.
- Signals used interchangeably: `see` where the proposition is stated directly, no signal where the support requires an inferential step, `cf.` as a general-purpose gesture.
- A string cite standing in for an argument.
- Secondary authority cited where primary authority exists and is on point.

### Drafting and register

- Legalese where a plain word works: *hereinafter, aforementioned, pursuant to, said party, witnesseth, the instant case.*
- Doublets and triplets carried by habit — *null and void*, *give, devise and bequeath* — where one word carries the meaning. Some pairs are genuine terms of art; most are scribal inheritance, and the test is whether removing one word changes an outcome.
- Obligations with no obligor: *reasonable efforts shall be made.*
- *And/or.* It is ambiguous, courts have said so repeatedly, and the fix is to write the three cases out.
- An effort standard nobody defined — *reasonable efforts*, *best efforts*, *commercially reasonable* — in an agreement that will be read by people who disagree about what it meant.
- A defined term used two ways, defined and never used, or capitalized without being defined at all.
- A client letter written in a judge's register.
- A question presented that is not a question, or that assumes its own answer.

## Write

Answer first. *Probably not*, then the reason, then what it turns on. The brief answer is the part the client reads, and burying it below the facts section serves the writer's sense of order rather than the reader's need.

Name a relevant fact, threshold or open question when known. State what evidence would help without claiming a particular record exists or would settle the issue unless that is established.

Identify the jurisdiction, governing law and, where relevant, standard of review. Ask for material missing context instead of silently assuming it.

Cite what the authority actually supports, with an available accurate locator. Check relevant subsequent history before relying on it in a professional document. If that check has not occurred or is unavailable, mark the validation need explicitly; do not claim a citator search was performed.

Choose the signal deliberately. No signal means the source states the proposition. `See` means the source supports it through an inferential step you expect the reader to make. `Cf.` means analogous but not on all fours. `But see` and `contra` do the same work in the other direction. Using them precisely is how a reader calibrates trust in the rest of the brief.

Address material adverse authority. For U.S. practice, ABA Model Rule 3.3(a)(2) concerns known, directly adverse authority in the controlling jurisdiction that opposing counsel has not disclosed; the jurisdiction's adopted rule governs the actual duty. A predictive memo should also assess material weaknesses rather than present only helpful sources.

In new drafting, make the actor, obligation and relevant timing explicit and flag unresolved terms for negotiation. In a copyedit, preserve the agreed obligor, effort standard, scope, dates, remedies and allocation of risk. Do not replace reasonable efforts with guaranteed performance, supply staffing commitments or add termination rights. A preference for *must* in new drafting does not authorize changing the legal effect of existing *shall* language.

Write the client letter for the client's actual knowledge. Explain necessary terms and give supported next steps, owners and deadlines; identify what remains unknown.

## Mechanics

Use the requested court or venue system. `domains/citation/bluebook22.md` carries its selected forms and verification limits; it does not control every client letter.

- Full citation on first reference, short form after, `id.` only for the source immediately preceding with no intervening authority.
- Accurate locators for specific propositions where available; a missing pincite is a verification need, not permission to create a page number.
- Case names italicized in text; the reporter, the court and the year in the parenthetical, with the court dropped where the reporter alone identifies it: `N.Y. Times Co. v. Sullivan, 376 U.S. 254, 279-80 (1964)` names no court because `U.S.` can only be the Supreme Court, while a circuit or state decision must say which one.
- Signals ordered and punctuated as the system requires, with a parenthetical explaining any citation whose relevance is not obvious from the proposition.
- Subsequent history appended where it affects validity.
- Block quotations, alterations and omissions handled under the applicable manual and local rules. Preserve quotation meaning and verify any disputed threshold rather than inferring it from an edition label.
- Defined terms capitalized consistently and used only as defined.
- Citation sentences and citation clauses punctuated as sentences and clauses, which is what distinguishes legal citation from every other system in `domains/citation/`.

## Formats

### Memo

Predictive. Question presented, brief answer, facts, discussion, conclusion.

Write the question presented so it contains the governing law, the legal question, and the two or three facts that will decide it. The brief answer commits to a probability and gives the reason in a sentence.

Rule, application and conclusion are a useful memo structure where they fit. Synthesize authorities when the task calls for it; a requested case survey may appropriately examine decisions in sequence.

Give independent, candid analysis rather than the desired answer. ABA Model Rule 2.1 expresses that principle for U.S. model-rule practice; verify the applicable professional rule.

### Brief

Persuasive, and adversarial in a system that expects it. Lead with the strongest ground rather than the most chronological one. State the standard of review early, because it often decides the case.

Frame the facts truthfully and favorably — those are compatible, and the skill of the genre lies in the gap between them. Answer the other side's best argument in its own terms rather than a weakened restatement.

Follow the governing tribunal's candor duties, including the applicable rules on adverse authority and correction of false material statements. Do not assume every court has adopted the ABA model text unchanged.

### Client letter

Use language the actual client can act on, with explained terms and appropriate source references. Formal citations are not automatically required or forbidden. IPLF's public [August 26, 2025 announcement](https://www.iplfederation.org/press-release-part-2/) describes ISO legal-communication guidance; its reader-oriented purpose is useful here, but this module does not claim to have verified the full normative text.

Say what the known situation means, what is recommended and what happens next. Include costs and dates when supplied or verified, and flag material unknowns. Do not let technical language obscure consequential information.

### Contract

Draft for a later reader who did not negotiate the agreement. Make actors, obligations, conditions and relevant timing clear. Use defined terms consistently without changing their scope.

Identify ambiguity in conjunctions, effort standards and consequences. In authorized new drafting, propose precise terms for review. In copyediting, do not decide which cases are intended, delete an effort standard or insert breach remedies; those choices can renegotiate the agreement.

## Evidence

The legacy `evidence_floor: 5` signals careful support appropriate to the claim, not a numerical probability requirement. A prediction may need a verbal qualification, and authority needs a current, jurisdiction-specific validity check. Sources can change in other fields too; law is not uniquely accountable for updates.

Bluebook signals express the relationship between authority and proposition. Every citation system still requires that the source support the claim; typography cannot supply missing authority.

Plainer words for a client with no legal training, and the same estimate underneath them. The pincite, the subsequent-history check and the calibration of the hedge are not register, so no tier on the ladder reaches them.

## Boundaries

With `stop-the-slop/voice.md`. There is no permission to erase meaningful qualifiers. Legal predictions, deliberate formality and terms of art remain when they do work; core's ordinary style preferences do not override substantive meaning.

Against `non-fiction`. Both cite densely, and the citations do different jobs. A note in a history tells the reader where a fact came from so they can go and check it. A legal citation asserts that an institution with power over the reader has already decided something, and its form encodes how much power. Legal history and biography of the bench are `non-fiction`; anything a client or a court will act on is this.

Against `medical`. The nearest neighbor in this directory. Both are regulated, both treat overstatement as a professional failure rather than a stylistic one, and both write simultaneously for a specialist and a lay reader. The difference is that medical writing's lay reader may act on it alone, while a client letter sits inside a continuing relationship in which questions can be asked.

With `domains/education-level/`. Describe the actual reader and task. A classroom preset can support a legal-studies assignment; a client's age or degree does not by itself call for an academic preset. Plain language preserves obligations and uncertainty.

## Safeguards

- Preserve the governing facts, jurisdiction, uncertainty and distinction between holding, dictum, allegation and inference.
- Do not invent authority, locators, subsequent-history checks, deadlines, costs or a prediction's basis. Identify missing legal verification explicitly.
- A copyedit preserves parties, duties, effort standards, dates, remedies and risk allocation. Put proposed substantive changes in separate review notes rather than silently renegotiating.
- Apply the actual jurisdiction's professional, procedural and disclosure rules. An ABA model rule or a citation manual is not a universal statement of governing law.
- Protect privileged and confidential information. Do not create public archives or share links to satisfy citation form.
- These safeguards can accompany another primary genre without importing Bluebook formatting or implying that qualified legal review has occurred.

## Examples

The cases, rules and financial records below are synthetic exercises where stated, not authorities to use in a real document. No subsequent-history or legal-research check is claimed.

**Certainty a lawyer cannot sell**

**Supplied facts:** The record contains a non-compete clause and the draft assurance below, but no governing jurisdiction, relevant employment facts or validated authorities. The task is to remove unsupported certainty, not predict the outcome from an invented case analysis.

> You will win this case. The non-compete is clearly unenforceable.

> We need the governing law, relevant employment facts and current authority before assessing enforceability. The clause alone does not support a promised outcome.

**Hedge that says nothing**

**Supplied facts:** A fictional rule requires disclosure if the fund held more than $10 million in assets on December 31. The amount is unknown and must be checked in its year-end statement. No response deadline or likelihood estimate is supplied.

> It is possible that the arrangement may potentially raise certain issues that could warrant further consideration.

> Disclosure depends on whether the fund held more than $10 million in assets on December 31. We need the year-end statement to apply that rule.

**Legalese for its own sake**

**Supplied facts:** Lessee is the agreement's defined party term. The existing obligation is to pay monthly rent by the first day of each month. The task is a copyedit, not a change to the party name or payment terms.

> Pursuant to the aforementioned agreement, Lessee shall hereinafter be obligated to remit payment on or before the first day of each calendar month.

> Lessee must pay rent by the first day of each month.

**Obligation with nobody in it**

**Supplied facts:** The agreement identifies Vendor as responsible for the migration under a reasonable-efforts standard. No completion date, staffing commitment, termination right or refund remedy is supplied. This is a grammar/clarity edit only.

> Reasonable efforts shall be made to complete the migration in a timely manner.

> Vendor must use reasonable efforts to complete the migration in a timely manner.

*Review note: the effort standard and timing may need clarification, but defining them would be a substantive drafting decision.*

**A case pushed past its holding**

**Supplied facts:** In the fictional Ruiz record, 512 F.3d 44 (6th Cir. 2008), page 51 concerns terms behind an unlabeled hyperlink below the fold. Page 53 note 7 expressly reserves agreements displaying terms on the page. The supplied client agreement displays its terms. No later decisions are supplied.

> Courts have held that click-through agreements are unenforceable. *Ruiz v. Marchetti Software*, 512 F.3d 44 (6th Cir. 2008).

> *Ruiz* declined to enforce the agreement with terms behind an unlabeled hyperlink below the fold. 512 F.3d 44, 51 (6th Cir. 2008). It expressly reserved agreements whose terms appear on the page. *Id.* at 53 n.7. Our agreement displays its terms, so this decision does not resolve its enforceability.

**Signal used carelessly**

**Supplied facts:** In this fictional exercise, Ruiz, 512 F.3d at 51, holds that unconscionability requires procedural and substantive elements. Alvarez v. Sentinel Mut., 604 F.3d 112, 119 (6th Cir. 2010), suggests in dicta that extreme procedural unfairness may suffice. These supplied records support the contrast, not a real-world legal conclusion.

> Contracts of adhesion are unenforceable in this circuit. *See* *Ruiz*, 512 F.3d at 51.

> Adhesion alone does not void a contract in this circuit; unconscionability requires both procedural and substantive elements. *Ruiz*, 512 F.3d at 51. *But see* *Alvarez v. Sentinel Mut.*, 604 F.3d 112, 119 (6th Cir. 2010) (suggesting in dicta that extreme procedural unfairness may suffice).
